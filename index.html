Our story

Many hands, one symphony

Our mark shows six hands moving together in a circle. That’s what a handmade piece is to us: many skills, generations of tradition and a lot of patience, coming together in harmony.

The Craft Symphony is our way of carrying that work from the maker’s hands to yours.
